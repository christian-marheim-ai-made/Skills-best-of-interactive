Interactive sketch of the walk-through illusion: what your character can see, cell by cell, as it walks through a portal. A ray through the opening sees the other world, a ray beside the portal sees this world, a ray into a pillar or a wall sees black. The world you are in flips as you cross the portal line, and at that instant everything that changes sits in the pillars' black shadow.
